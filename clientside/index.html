Coffs Coast Charity Collective

Connecting our community through charity events that raise funds and awareness for local causes. Every ticket is a donation. Every event makes a difference.

Our Mission

The Coffs Coast Charity Collective is a not-for-profit organisation based in Coffs Harbour, New South Wales. We partner with local charities, volunteer groups and community organisations to run fundraising events that bring people together for good causes.

From fun runs along Coffs Creek to gala dinners at Opal Cove Resort, our events raise essential funds for children's health, marine conservation, and animal welfare across the Coffs Coast region.
